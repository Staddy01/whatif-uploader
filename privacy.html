Privacy Policy

WhatIf Channel Uploader is used only by the owner of one YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services to upload videos, set video details and thumbnails, and read analytics for that owner's own channel.

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No data from other users is collected.

By using this app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.
